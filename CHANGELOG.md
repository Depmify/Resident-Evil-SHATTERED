# RESIDENT EVIL: SHATTERED

## v0.1.6 — Harassment Difficulty & Multiplayer Improvements

SHATTERED v0.1.6 adds a new Story Mode difficulty, brings new threats into the game, and improves the multiplayer and mod experience. This update also includes fixes for several multiplayer issues.

### What's New for Players

- Take on Story Mode's new Harassment difficulty.
- Find the Benelli in Richard's room.
- Encounter new appearances from Mr. X and Nemesis.
- Use improved support for compatible mods.
- Navigate the multiplayer menus more easily.
- Benefit from fixes for several multiplayer issues.

## v0.1.5 — Complete Online Co-op, Mods & New Combat

SHATTERED v0.1.5 brings online co-op to the complete campaign. Play through the story with a friend from beginning to end, use compatible mods, and face a new range of enemies and weapons inspired by Resident Evil 2 and Resident Evil 3.

### What's New for Players

- Play through the complete campaign online with a friend, from the opening to the ending.
- Use compatible mods with SHATTERED.
- Face Resident Evil 2 zombies, replacing the original Resident Evil 1 zombies.
- Use weapons from Resident Evil 3, including the Western Custom, Benelli M3, and Eagle.
- Evade enemy attacks with the Resident Evil 3 dodge.
- Unlock a special skin by completing the game for the first time.
- Benefit from numerous bug fixes and gameplay improvements.

## v0.1.0 — Jill's Story, From Start to Finish

This release brings Jill's campaign from the Spencer Mansion through its ending, with more of the story playable together in SplitScreen.

### What's New for Players

- Play through Jill's story from beginning to end, including the later areas and the finale.
- Experience selected cutscenes and story moments together in cooperative SplitScreen.
- Continue further through the campaign together, with improvements to cooperative encounters, puzzles, interactions, and story progression.
- Feel gameplay through controller vibration.
- Enjoy improved movement and controller response during exploration and combat.
- Start from a refreshed main menu and use an updated launcher to set up the game.
- See and hear more of the campaign through improvements to story sequences, sound, and the finale.

### Multiplayer Availability

Native online and LAN multiplayer are disabled and blocked until further notice. Cooperative play is available through SplitScreen. For remote play, players can use a third-party tool such as Parsec to share a SplitScreen session; this is not a built-in SHATTERED feature.

## v0.0.3 — First Mansion Visit Test

This experimental release added network play and expanded the verified co-op route through the first visit to Spencer Mansion.

### What's Changed

- Added experimental online and LAN multiplayer for sessions of up to two players, with improved lobby stability and a reconnect window.
- Expanded Jill and Barry's co-op route through the first Spencer Mansion visit, up to the exit to the courtyard. Later areas and campaign events are not yet adapted or verified for co-op.
- Improved synchronization for cooperative puzzles and room events, including the shotgun trap sequence.
- Added the RE1.5 inventory option and its gameplay interface.
- Added RE3-inspired character movement, smoother combat transitions, and an emergency dodge.
- Added the RE3 M4 and grenade launcher, including gameplay, visuals, effects, and sounds.
- Expanded enemy and boss behavior across encounters including Hunters, Plant 42, Neptune, Black Tiger, Web Spinners, Wasps, and Adders.
- Added a full-screen gameplay pause menu and improved launcher settings persistence and controller mapping.
- Fixed cooperative room transitions, pickups, combat interactions, and scripted sequences.

At the time, this was an experimental build. Online and LAN play could have bugs, disconnections, or synchronization issues.

The online and LAN modes described in these v0.0.3 notes were later disabled and were not available in v0.1.0. Online co-op is available again in v0.1.5; see that release's notes for the current feature set.

## v0.0.2 — Changes Since v0.0.1

This update expands the early public test build from a launcher and setup experience into a broader playable survival-horror test build.

### What's Changed

- The main menu has been expanded into a clearer starting point for choosing how you want to play.
- The options screens now provide a more complete way to adjust display, language, frame rate, and controls before starting.
- A dedicated multiplayer menu is now visible, but it is not functional yet; SplitScreen is currently the only working multiplayer mode.
- Cooperative play now reaches further into the mansion instead of stopping at the initial setup flow.
- Versus mode lets players turn the mansion into a direct survival-horror competition.
- Tyrant Battle provides a focused battle experience built around facing the Tyrant in a dedicated encounter.
- Room transitions, shared scenes, rescues, deaths, endings, and the map now behave more consistently during extended sessions.
- Inventory handling, equipment, ammunition, the bazooka, and the rocket launcher have been expanded to give players more ways to prepare and fight.
- Crow, Yawn, Tyrant, Cerberus, Plant, and Zombie encounters have been added or refined with more dependable movement, attacks, reactions, and sounds.
- Room puzzles, pickups, story moments, fixed-camera scenes, animation, music, sound effects, and controller response have received broader support across the test build.
