Why Shattered?

Classic fixed-camera horror was built to isolate you.

SHATTERED asks what happens when that fear becomes shared: when two players step into the same mansion, split attention across the room, and turn survival-horror tension into a co-op memory.

The goal is not to replace the original feeling. It is to preserve the mood, friction, and strange quiet of classic survival horror while opening a new way to experience it with a friend.

Current state

Built for testing, not for polished play.

Available

Public test build v0.0.3 with co-op through the first Spencer Mansion visit, experimental online/LAN for up to two players, and a Windows launcher plus client package.

Multiplayer status

Online/LAN multiplayer supports sessions of up to two players and remains experimental. Expect bugs, disconnections, or synchronization issues.

Experimental modes

The update adds expanded gameplay options, RE3-inspired movement and weapons, an emergency dodge, and new enemy encounters.

Private

The active source code, internal tools, research branches, and development assets are maintained outside this repository.

v0.0.3 changelog

What changed for players

Full release notes
  • Experimental online and LAN multiplayer supports sessions of up to two players, with improved lobby stability and a reconnect window.
  • Jill and Barry's verified co-op route covers the first Spencer Mansion visit, up to the courtyard exit; later areas are not yet adapted or verified.
  • Cooperative puzzles and room events have improved synchronization, including the shotgun trap sequence.
  • The RE1.5 inventory option and its gameplay interface are now available.
  • Playable characters gain RE3-inspired movement, smoother combat transitions, and an emergency dodge.
  • The RE3 M4 and grenade launcher are added, with new enemy and boss behavior across several encounters.
  • A full-screen pause menu, launcher settings persistence, controller mapping, and co-op fixes are included.

Support us

Classic horror is better when it can be shared.

Depmify is a fan developer focused on bringing classic survival horror experiences closer to players who want to explore them together. Alongside RESIDENT EVIL: SHATTERED, Depmify also develops Silent Hill: United, an unofficial multiplayer project for Silent Hill 1 built around preserving atmosphere while adding shared presence.
