    HOW TO PLAY
    1. Downloadthe latest release from GitHub.
    2. Extractit to its own folder. Your game data stays where it is.
    3. Start the launcherand select your Resident Evil 1 PC folder.
    4. Press START GAMEthen pick SINGLEPLAYER, COOPERATIVE (SplitScreen) or MULTIPLAYER in the game.

    TROUBLESHOOTING
    SHATTERED.exe was not found

    Keep SHATTERED.exe in the release folder, next to Launcher.exe.

    Black screen, no movies, rooms fail to load

    Point the launcher at the root of a complete installation: Data, Movie, Sound, Voice, Stage1-7, enemy, ITEM_M1, Item_m2 and Effspr.

    Crashes or startup errors

    Send Crash.log (next to SHATTERED.exe, or in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Resident Evil SHATTERED) with your report.

    F3FPS and infoF11fullscreen- change any key in OPTIONS > CONTROLS

    Why Shattered?

    Classic fixed-camera horror was built to isolate you. SHATTERED asks what happens when that fear becomes shared.

    Two players step into the same mansion, split their attention across the room, and turn survival-horror tension into a co-op memory.

    The goal is not to replace the original feeling. It is to keep the mood, the friction and the strange quiet, while opening a new way to live it with a friend.
